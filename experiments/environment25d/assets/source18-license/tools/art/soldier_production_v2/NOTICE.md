https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/

- Original author release: https://durian.blender.org/news/guardians-community-help/
- Original package: https://durian.blender.org/wp-content/content/guardians_r854.zip
- Rights-holder published-data grant: https://durian.blender.org/sharing/
- Original ZIP SHA256: `dd0796eba8f2b08a239470488559f3a1b2ccd7b1140fdb93360bbb177d4776b9`

This is the early 2010 r854 work-in-progress, not film-final art. The original
ZIP has no separate LICENSE; the official rights-holder grant covers its published
data. No endorsement is implied. Separate external reference photographs are
not imported. Embedded rig UI scripts are never executed by these production tools.

Changes: complete sewn coat geometry/UV fitted to a natural adult skeleton,
retailored into a longer crossing-front military undergarment, sleeve taper,
walking splits, new skin weights, runtime LOD, real surface-relief normal bake,
new controlled material, and a separately authored continuous armor outfit.
Original professional source remains separate and unmodified.

certification. Current derivative alters lower-leg fit, palette and skinning.

**punkduck medieval boots: CC BY 4.0**:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
Source: https://static.makehumancommunity.org/assets/assetpacks/shoes03.html

skeleton weights and controlled cloth-shoe palette. Credit accompanies derivatives.

not used as the current rendered undergarment and explicitly excluded from GLB.
Disputed-license Viking assets are not included.
