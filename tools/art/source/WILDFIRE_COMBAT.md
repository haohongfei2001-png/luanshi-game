# Wildfire complete armed animation source

Author is Wildfire Games and contributors. Original editable files are public COLLADA sources from 0 A.D., pinned at 61a3b9507d974084e6badb88a0826bd89a6d5b8b.

Source paths and Git blob identities are fixed in `tools/art/import_wildfire_combat.py`. The selected overhead cut is `binaries/data/mods/public/art/animation/biped/infantry/swordsman/attack_melee_shield_02.dae`, blob 32ace1da919078a25f156a0fbb80131af477e1ff.

The matching ready stance is `binaries/data/mods/public/art/animation/biped/infantry/swordsman/idle_ready_shield_01.dae`, blob fbbb12bcef7b5cb1a409596d7db293de160c080c, from the same pinned source and license.

The full parent/child source matrices, bind matrices and weighted source boot vertices are sampled offline. The derived curves and any animation palette containing them are distributed under CC BY-SA 3.0. Existing independently authored meshes, textures and source clips keep their recorded licenses.

The JSON retains the two selected editable curves and weighted source-boot sole samples. Rejected source studies remain in the research evidence branch. Ground sword and dao basic attacks use the complete ready, hip, support-leg, shoulder and wrist chain at their existing distinct simulation timings. Mounted basic strips preserve the previous pose. Other weapon actions remain unchanged. This limited integration does not establish acceptance of the complete game art.
