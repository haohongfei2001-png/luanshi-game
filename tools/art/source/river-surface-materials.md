# River-gate ground and inland canopy

These three original game textures were generated for this repository with OpenAI ImageGen. They contain no external game art, character, logo or photograph. No image of the battle was used as scenery. The Godot field shader projects the first two over the existing road and soil map; the third is sampled by curved, world-oriented branch meshes. The separate willow resource is preserved. Mipmaps are generated on import. The battle renderer and the gameplay camera produce all acceptance images.

### Grazed meadow

Use case: stylized-concept. Production RGB ALBEDO TEXTURE for an actual Chinese historical 3D tactics game, a square seamless material, not a game screenshot. The entire square is a straight-down orthographic sample of about 7 by 7 meters of trampled late summer alluvial river meadow in northern China. Restrained hand-authored animation background art of the quality of a classic high-end MOBA. Clear, deliberate, grouped grass shapes, broad clean painted color planes. Dominant desaturated cool moss-jade and green-umber turf, intermittent dull straw-green, a few low flattened bunches, fragments of grey green shale close to ground and patches of exposed warm brown earth. Around two thirds is quiet short grass and earthy negative space; a few varied clusters of thin tapering leaves and muted sage herbs give authentic human-scale detail, rather than all-over tiny marks. Subtle stone facets with dark contact edges. Crisp clean silhouettes, modest tonal range with mid-dark base for light figures, flat even diffuse daylight, no strong cast shadows. No trees, trails, water, horizon, trunks, hills, overhead canopy or individual humans. Not an illustration of scenery: a tileable texture covering the whole image, all four edges continue naturally, equal-scale details edge to edge, no square borders, no labels, no UI, no text, no cast directional shadows. No photogrammetry, no noisy strokes, no pixel art, no plastic, no ornamental leaf pattern or repeating stamps. Square 1024x1024, full bleed.

### Loess wagonway

Use case: stylized-concept. A production color/albedo texture tile for the ROAD SURFACE of a playable stylized historical Chinese tactics game. Perfectly vertical orthographic top-down view onto 5 metres of old compacted warm reddish-brown loess and clay road, trampled by horses and carts. ONLY the road material fills the whole square. Quiet broad bare patches of packed ochre umber, shallow elongated worn strips in the vertical direction, embedded varied grey shale splinters, small gravel deposits in irregular pockets, thin hardened ledges from soil broken along wheel wear, occasional darker soil in shallow channels. Subtle gravel smaller than a boot, not cobblestone pavement. Late-summer daytime, smooth brush rendering with strong authored shape design, 2–3 crisp but quiet color planes on fragments, small grounded contact ambient occlusion, no heavy shadows. Darker than pale sand, clay from muted ochre through raw sienna to cool grey brown; native-sized game figures need to pop. Think sophisticated East Asian animation background painted for a readable overhead game. Medium-scale earthiness, avoid all-over dirt noise, photogrammetry and smooth gradients. Seamless repeating sides, homogeneous scale, no horizon, perspective, diagonal asphalt, tree, grass, pavement edges, footprints as symbols, horseshoe prints as repeating patterns, HUD, people, writing or labels. It is a single asset image completely filled with the same material. Square.

### Elm bough

Use case: stylized-concept. An ORIGINAL transparent-background production texture for a 3D game tree, one single horizontal spreading branch spray from a Chinese elm, seen overhead from slightly above. The composition resembles a flattened luxuriant botanical fan, with a small stem entering from bottom-center then splitting densely upward and out to both sides. The leafy bough spans most of the entire image with a shallow profile, highest in the middle. Silhouette is serrated with hundreds of small recognizable pointed leaves which are grouped as larger overlapping clusters, a few crossed twigs and natural irregular tiny meshed openings, silhouette extremely full through the middle, wild and distributed differently across the two sides. Paint with superb historical Chinese animation background color design for a bright readable 3D tactics landscape: deep forest teal shade, medium dark olive green midplanes, small golden sage tips; three clean tonal levels and subtle natural strokes. Avoid lime green, enormous individual leaves, heavy black outlines, decorative symmetric calligraphy, photorealism. Even diffuse summer daylight, subtle value shift at top of leaf clumps, no hard cast shadow, no ground, no fluffy blobs, no sphere or entire tree, no trunk or horizon. Isolated foliage cluster on genuine fully transparent background, no edge halo, no borders labels or text. Square sheet; leafy fan nearly touching upper and side margins with short woody stem ending near the bottom. This will be mapped onto overlapping hand-sculpted branches in an actual running Godot game; make the bough beautiful in silhouette at 50 pixels across.
