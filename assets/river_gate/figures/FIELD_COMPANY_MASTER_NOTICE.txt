River Gate complete character master — source and adaptation

The anatomical foundation is MakeHuman Community HM08 base.obj, default.mhskel
and default_weights.mhw at revision a8bc2d54ff0ac92e78ff71431b1023eda42bf482.
The upstream asset files identify CC0 1.0. The original author was Manuel Bastioni. The named upstream rights holders
are Data Collection AB, Joel Palmius and Jonas Hauquier.
https://github.com/makehumancommunity/makehuman/blob/a8bc2d54ff0ac92e78ff71431b1023eda42bf482/LICENSE.ASSETS.md
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Morph inputs from the same release are asian-male-young.target,
male-young-averagemuscle-averageweight-idealproportions.target and
universal-male-young-maxmuscle-averageweight.target.
Exact source blob IDs and weights are checked by tools/art/bake_field_anatomy.py.
The game adaptation fits the original surface to its field animation joints,
closes the eye/mouth apertures for the battle camera, and derives three
reductions. Feet use the source anatomical last under closed leather.
Hands remain the existing separately licensed HM08 fitted grips.

The garments are Wildfire Games 0 A.D. m_dress_sleeves.dae and
m_armor_tunic_long_sleeves.dae, and the existing attributed Han headwear.
These are publicly editable game art from revision
61a3b9507d974084e6badb88a0826bd89a6d5b8b, distributed CC BY-SA 3.0.

Wildfire Games — https://www.wildfiregames.com/
The COLLADA files identify their authoring tool as Blender 2.80 and the author
field as Blender User. Attribution is to Wildfire Games and its contributors.
No more specific personal authorship is asserted.
The derivative garments retain their original surfaces, UVs and skin weights,
with retargeted joints, fitted hems and existing Han cloth/lamellar colour maps.
Their source blob IDs are recorded in tools/art/mature_field_company.py.
Existing garment and headwear notices continue to identify the maps and helms.

The combined field_company_master.glb contains these CC BY-SA 3.0 garment
adaptations, CC0 anatomical components and the project's original mantle.
The combined master is provided under CC BY-SA 3.0. The separate original
assets retain their respective licenses. No gameplay license is changed.
