Luanshi modifications sample the complete authored chain, retarget its hips,
supporting legs, shoulder and elbow planes, and fit two palms to one unchanged
rigid spear. The original loaded beginning is the ready stance. The source
maximum extension aligns to the existing simulation release; timing, damage
and mounted spear poses retain existing game behavior.
