# Rejected full armor study and preserved source

The new `approved_spearman_kit.py` name means **inspired by the owner-approved
reference**, not that the produced model is approved. Three local whole-body
Blender trials failed: disconnected shoulder pieces; then a rectangular chest
bib/open shoulders; then rigid bucket-like shoulder armor when lowering arms.
183k source faces and sub-micrometre rigid rest placement do not fix silhouette.
No trial was accepted for shipping or depicted as Godot gameplay.

`young_head_module.py` preserves complete original163-bone MakeHuman source
alongside Guardian308 bones,471 source bones total. First head inherited hidden
render state; second was oversized; third corrected fit. This is an editable
source experiment, not a471-bone runtime requirement or finalized protagonist.
`spearman_helmet.py` first had forehead penetration; second improved centering,
but does not complete the armor, shoes, puttees, grip or motion acceptance.

`recut_front.py` now fixes legacy eyebrow hair radius (10mm accidental roots to
70µm authored roots). A connected18cm hem extension failed with pants penetration;
it remains opt-in `--rejected-hem`, not part of the default derived garment.
Original mature source geometry/UV/rig remain recoverable through the pinned
Sintel source recovery and MakeHuman source manifests. Never reuse the old1373
vertex cage response on these changed garment indices.

All images and .blend trials remain locally under
`character-diagnostics/sintel-guardian/{approved-kit1,approved-kit2,approved-kit3,
young-head-kit1,young-head-kit2,young-head-kit3,helmet-kit1,helmet-kit2,recut-front9}`.
They are internal rejected Blender studies, not actual Godot screenshots.
The approved visual target now emphasizes natural matte volume, coherent chest/
shoulder overlap and readable hands/weapons at MOBA scale. Microhole polishing
of this rejected silhouette is not the production route.
